# Product Overview

Upright Systems Inc. is a Philippine-based IT solutions company website. This is a modern, single-page marketing site showcasing the company's services, history, and capabilities.

## Key Features

- Single-page application with smooth scroll navigation
- Dark/light theme toggle
- Video background hero section with carousel
- Company timeline (2015-2025)
- Service offerings showcase
- Industries served visualization
- Contact form with validation
- Fully responsive design (mobile-first)

## Target Audience

Business clients seeking IT integration, software development, professional services, and hardware solutions across multiple industries.

## Brand Identity

- Primary color: Blue (oklch(0.45 0.15 250))
- Accent color: Yellow (oklch(0.85 0.15 85))
- Professional, modern aesthetic
- Focus on enterprise-scale solutions
